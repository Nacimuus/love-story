Example locations

Illustrative venues for design testing — not real redemption partners.

Location and privacy. “Find places near me” asks your browser for permission and calculates distances locally. You can refuse GPS access and still browse the map. No exact coordinates are sent to Plausible or to the site operator by this demo. Opening the map loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, which may infer the displayed area from tile requests. Any future voluntary sharing of exact coordinates with the gift-card team must be presented as a distinct, clearly explained action.
